Ashish Kujur

School of Mathematics, IISER Thiruvananthapuram

I am a fourth-year PhD student in mathematics at IISER Thiruvananthapuram. My advisor is Md. Ramiz Reza (Aliah University, Kolkata), and my institutional advisor is Sheetal Dharmatti.

My research lies at the intersection of operator theory and complex function theory. I study Hilbert spaces of analytic functions, especially Dirichlet-type and de Branges–Rovnyak spaces, as well as weighted shifts on directed trees.
